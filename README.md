One funny one-line joke about office life, judged head to head in pairs. Free to enter.

The entry (116 characters):

> My desk plant died on a Tuesday, and Facilities sent a sympathy card signed by the whole floor, including the plant.

A desk plant dies and the office treats it like a colleague — Facilities sends a sympathy card signed by the whole floor, and the punch is that the plant signed it too.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, no trailing newline). Override the output path with `ENTRY_OUTPUT` if the harness needs a different location:

```sh
ENTRY_OUTPUT=/tmp/entry.txt python3 main.py
```
